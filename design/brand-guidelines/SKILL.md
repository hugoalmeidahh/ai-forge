# Brand guidelines

Use the user's brand, not the identity of a model vendor. Separate supplied facts/assets, observed conventions and proposed choices. Confirm missing identity decisions only when they materially affect the work.

## Workflow

1. Collect authorized logo variants, palette, type, voice, audience, product vocabulary and required formats. Inspect current usage and asset licenses.
2. Define roles and constraints: logo clear space/minimum reproduction, contrast-safe color pairs, type hierarchy/fallbacks, image treatment and tone examples.
3. Map visual roles to existing design tokens when producing UI. Keep source values and generated exports aligned; do not maintain independent conflicting palettes.
4. Draft copy from the audience's task and use consistent action names. Preserve supplied claims; flag unsupported metrics, testimonials or affiliations.
5. Produce only requested deliverables and review across their real sizes, themes and media. Identify which variants are approved facts versus proposals.

Deliver a concise usable guide plus affected assets/examples and attribution. Publishing, sending a campaign or purchasing assets requires the task's existing authorization; authoring a guide does not authorize those actions.
